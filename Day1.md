Q. How to make the DevOps teams understand the importance of Security at their firm level?
⇒ Make them understand the prone risk for vulnerabilities and loopholes via the <u>Threat Modeling</u> demonstrations on their system.

→ Open Source tool which can used here is the OWASP <u>Threat Dragron</u> v2.5.0

- OWASP thread dragon uses the framework “STRIDE” made by MicroSoft.
- S = Spoofing Identity; impersonating a user or system
- T= Tampering with Data; altering or modifying data
- R= Repudiation ; denying actions or transactions
- I= Information Disclosure; exposing sensitive data
- D= Denial of Service; disrupting service avaliability
- E= Elevation of Privilege; gaining unauthorized access
